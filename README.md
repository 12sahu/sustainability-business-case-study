# 🌍 Sustainability-Driven Revenue Optimization

### 📊 A Data-Driven Business Case Study

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Businesses today face:

* Increasing **waste generation**
* Rising **operational costs**
* Growing pressure for **sustainability**

👉 **Key Question:**
Can sustainability initiatives reduce waste while improving business revenue?

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

* Reduce **waste generation**
* Improve **sustainability practices**
* Increase **revenue through smarter strategies**

---

## 📊 Dataset

This project uses two datasets:

* 🛒 **E-commerce dataset** → Revenue, customers, products
* 🌱 **CO₂ emissions dataset** → Environmental impact across countries

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 📈 Business Analysis

* Revenue trends over time
* Top products and customers
* Country-wise revenue distribution

### 🌱 Sustainability Analysis

* Emissions by country
* Emissions by sector
* Environmental impact trends

### 🔗 Combined Analysis

* Compared **revenue vs carbon emissions**
* Identified relationship between **consumption and sustainability**

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

* **Total Revenue**
* **Monthly Revenue Growth**
* **Top Customers Contribution**
* **CO₂ Emissions by Country**
* **Sector-wise Emissions**

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* A small number of countries drive the majority of **revenue**
* High-revenue regions often show higher **carbon emissions**
* The **power sector** is a major contributor to emissions
* Customer spending is highly concentrated among top buyers
* **Sustainable product categories** show strong growth potential

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

Sustainability is not just an environmental concern—it is a **business opportunity**.
Companies that adopt sustainable practices can achieve **long-term growth and efficiency**.
